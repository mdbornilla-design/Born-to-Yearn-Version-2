FAQ

Common questions.

How do I track my order?

Go to the Orders page, enter your order ID (for example BY-1005) and you'll see each step: placed, packed, shipped, out for delivery and delivered.

Which payment methods can I use?

GCash, credit/debit card, bank transfer and cash on delivery are available at checkout. This demo store doesn't collect any real payment.

Is shipping free?

Yes for orders over ₱5,000 after discounts. Smaller orders pay a flat ₱250.

Do you have voucher codes?

Yes — try BORN10 for 10% off, or YEARN500 for ₱500 off orders of ₱10,000 or more. Enter the code in your bag before checkout.

How do I pick the right laptop or monitor?

Open any product page for its full specs and related gear. Not sure? Send us a message below and we'll help you choose.

Can I return an item?

You can return unused items in their original packaging within 7 days of delivery. Contact us with your order ID to start a return.

Are the specs and prices final?

Specs are typical for each model and can vary by configuration. Prices and stock on this site are sample values — confirm before ordering.

Does the site save my wishlist and bag?

Yes, on this device. Your bag, saved items and orders are stored in your browser, so clearing site data will remove them.
